Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a stay?

Use the inquiry form above, call us at (248) 692-7831, or book a specific home online from the Stay & Board page. For multiple homes or the whole farm, reach out directly so we can hold everything together.

How many people can stay at Narrin Farms?

Our four homes sleep up to 23 guests in total: The Lodge (9), The Clubhouse (6), The Woods (6) and The Grooms (2).

Are pets allowed?

Yes — the Clubhouse and the Woods are pet friendly. Please keep dogs leashed around the farm animals and the pastures. Ask us about the other homes.

Can we use the lake?

Absolutely. Narrin Lake is a private-access, spring-fed lake with no motorsports — perfect for kayaking, paddling and fishing. Kayaks are available to guests.

Can we see or meet the animals?

The farm is home to horses, donkeys, cows (including a Texas longhorn), sheep, pigs and chickens. You’ll see them in the pastures and barnyard; we’re building out more ways for guests to interact with the horses, so ask what’s available during your stay.

Do you offer horse boarding?

Yes. Narrin Farms is a working equestrian farm with pastures and wooded trails. Spots are limited — call (248) 692-7831 for current availability, care options and rates.

What is there to do nearby?

Holly State Recreation Area is about 0.7 miles from the Woods, with trails, beaches and mountain biking. Mt. Holly ski area is close by for winter, and the villages of Ortonville, Holly and Clarkston offer restaurants, coffee and shops.
