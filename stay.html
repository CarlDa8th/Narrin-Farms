The Clubhouse

Panoramic views from the hill.

The Clubhouse earned its name as the place young riders gathered on breaks from their horses. Today it sits on a hill with panoramic views of the lake and pastures — with a pergola, a stone patio and a ring of red Adirondack chairs around the fire pit.

Weddings & events

Say “I do” by the lake.

Narrin Farms is a working equestrian farm overlooking Narrin Lake — and one of the prettiest places in Oakland County to get married. Our weddings are intimate and unhurried, with your closest people staying right here on the property.

Get ready in the Lodge, walk down a grass aisle with the water behind you, toast under the café lights and end the night around the fire. In the morning, everyone wakes up on the farm for one more coffee together.

Horse boarding

Room to graze. Trails to ride.

Horses have always been the heart of Narrin Farms — the Clubhouse got its name from young riders gathering between rides. We offer boarding on our working equestrian farm in Ortonville, with pastures, wooded trails and a herd that already feels like family.
